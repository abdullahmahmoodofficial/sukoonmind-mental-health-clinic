About SukoonMind

Sukoon means peace, calm, rest. That is what we try to build here — one honest conversation at a time.

Why we exist

In Pakistan, too many people carry stress, grief and anxiety alone — because talking about mental health still feels difficult, or because help feels far away and expensive. SukoonMind opened in Gulberg, Lahore, to change that: a welcoming place where asking for support is normal, private and affordable.

We keep our promises small and real: private sessions, clear prices, therapists who listen before they advise, and no inflated claims about what therapy can do.

  • Private rooms and private video calls — never shared spaces
  • Clear PKR fees, confirmed before you pay anything
  • Urdu and English sessions, for all ages from teens to elders
  • Practical tools you can use between sessions, not just talk

What a visit feels like

Calm from the door onwards.

A quiet welcome

Soft light, a comfortable waiting area, and no crowded reception desk. Arrive a few minutes early and settle in.

Your own room

Every session happens in a private room, one therapist and one client — or one family — at a time.

Tools to take home

Breathing exercises, journaling prompts and small weekly practices, so progress continues between sessions.

HOW A SESSION WORKS

One conversation at a time

  1. Book privately — pick a time, a therapist focus and in-person or online. First name only is fine.
  2. Talk, at your pace — 50 minutes, just you and your therapist. No judgement, no rush.
  3. Leave with a plan — practical tools for the week, and a next session whenever you are ready.
